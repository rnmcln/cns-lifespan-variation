# Data

| Directory | Contents | Redistribution |
|---|---|---|
| `gbd/` | Global Burden of Disease 2023 extracts: the official theoretical minimum risk life table, the schedule derived from published outputs, and the global CNS extract used to derive it | IHME free-of-charge non-commercial terms |
| `raw_hmd/` | empty; Human Mortality Database series are retrieved by `code/fetch_hmd.py` | not redistributable |
| `derived/` | every dataset generated by the pipeline, including the country-year results and the file manifest | CC BY 4.0, except where computed from Human Mortality Database series |

`derived/results_countryyear.csv` is the country-year level dataset underlying all reported
results; `derived/data_dictionary_countryyear.csv` defines its variables and units.

`derived/hmd_file_manifest.csv` records the series vintage, file size and full
SHA-256 digest of each of the 140 Human Mortality Database files used, so that a fresh
retrieval can be checked against the versions that produced the reported results.

`derived/asyr_change_country.csv` gives, for each country, the age-standardised YLL rate under the
national, GBD 2023 and minimum-mortality references, the absolute and percentage change between
them, and national life expectancy at birth.

`derived/reversal_pairs.csv` lists every pair of the 28 countries with their age-standardised YLL
rates under the national and GBD 2023 references, the gap between them as an absolute difference
and as a percentage of the pair mean, whether the ordering reverses, and the proportion of
Poisson replicates in which each ordering and the reversal recur. `derived/rank_by_cause.csv`
gives the rank statistics for all nine causes on the common country frame.
`derived/cause_age_deaths.csv` holds the Eurostat age-specific deaths for the nine causes; the
matching exposures and remaining life expectancies are computed from Human Mortality Database
series and are not distributed.
